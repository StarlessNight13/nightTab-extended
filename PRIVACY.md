# NightlyTab privacy policy

**Last updated: 2026-10-01**

NightlyTab is an independent, unofficial fork of nightTab. This policy describes the source in this repository as of the date above. Update it if a later release changes these practices.

## Data stored on your device

NightlyTab stores bookmarks, layout and theme settings, and other configuration in the browser's local storage. Uploaded background images are stored in the browser's IndexedDB. NightlyTab does not send this saved data to its maintainers or to a NightlyTab-operated server.

When you choose **Export**, NightlyTab creates a backup file on your device. The backup can include bookmarks, settings, themes, and uploaded background images. You decide where to save or share that file.

## Requests to other services

- When you submit a search, your search terms are sent directly to the search provider selected in NightlyTab.
- NightlyTab may request Google Fonts for the fonts selected in its theme settings.
- User-entered background image and video URLs, and user-provided bookmark images, may be loaded directly from their hosting services. Preset backgrounds use built-in theme colors.

Those service providers receive the network requests needed to return the requested search results or media and may process request information under their own privacy policies. NightlyTab does not receive the submitted search terms or remote media requests.

## Analytics and accounts

NightlyTab has no account system, analytics, advertising, or telemetry. The extension requests no browser permissions beyond replacing the new-tab page.

## Contact

For privacy questions about this fork, use the [NightlyTab repository](https://github.com/StarlessNight13/nightTab-extended) or its issue tracker.
