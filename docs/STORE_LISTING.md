## Short description

Customize your new tab with bookmark groups, layouts, themes, and backgrounds.

## Full description

NightlyTab is an independent, unofficial GPL-3.0 fork of nightTab by zombieFox. It replaces the browser's new-tab page with a customizable start page for bookmarks, groups, themes, fonts, and backgrounds.

NightlyTab includes additional fork-specific bookmark and background-image controls. Settings are stored locally in your browser, and you can export a backup when you choose. Searches are sent directly to the search provider you select. Fonts and remote background or bookmark images may be fetched from their hosting services.

NightlyTab is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original nightTab project. Source code and license: https://github.com/StarlessNight13/nightTab-extended
